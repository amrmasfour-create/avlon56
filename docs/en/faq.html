FAQ

Do you sell factory (OEM) screens?

No. We only supply aftermarket Android screens installed after the car is built.

Will my steering wheel buttons still work?

In most cars yes. Each car page shows whether they are supported, need an adapter, or are not supported.

What does "compatible with modifications" mean?

The screen fits but needs an extra part or adapter; the required modifications are listed clearly.

Does my factory reverse camera keep working?

Usually yes through the right adapter; the car page details camera and sensor support.

How long does installation take?

Typically one to three hours depending on the car and its equipment.
